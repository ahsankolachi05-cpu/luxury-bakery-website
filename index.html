Our story

Baked slowly,
finished by hand.

“A cake should feel like a keepsake — beautiful enough to photograph, delicious enough to remember.”

Maison Blanche began as a home atelier with one belief: celebrations deserve cakes made with the same care as the moments themselves. Every sponge is baked in small batches, every petal placed by hand, every ribbon tied at the last moment.

We work closely with each client from first sketch to final delivery, so what arrives at your table feels unmistakably yours.
